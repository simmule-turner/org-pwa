#+TITLE: Attachments
#+STARTUP: showall

Where a heading keeps its files, and what attaching one writes. The results are what Emacs 29.3 (Org 9.6.15) gave for the outline below, kept in =/home/me/org/notes.org=; the app gives the same where the last section does not say otherwise. Choosing files, the camera and recording are the app's own and are not here.

The outline:
#+BEGIN_SRC org
,* Taxes :work:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 7a3b9c4e-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d
:END:
,** Form 1040
,** Receipts
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 11223344-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d
:END:
,* Trips
:PROPERTIES:
:DIR: files/trips
:END:
,** Packing list
,* Ideas
#+END_SRC

* Where the files go
A heading with an =ID= keeps its files in =data/= with the first two characters of the ID as a folder, and the rest as a folder under it. A =DIR= property names a folder of your choosing, relative to the file. A heading with neither has no folder, and a sub-heading does not use its parent's.

- =Taxes= :: data/7a/3b9c4e-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d
- =Form 1040= :: (none)
- =Receipts= :: data/11/223344-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d
- =Trips= :: files/trips
- =Packing list= :: (none)
- =Ideas= :: (none)

* Attaching
Attaching a file copies it into the folder and puts the =ATTACH= tag on the heading. The files are listed with their folder; the outline is shown after the last file is attached.

Attach =w2.pdf= to =Taxes=:
#+BEGIN_SRC org
,* Taxes                                                         :work:ATTACH:
,** Form 1040
,** Receipts
,* Trips
,** Packing list
,* Ideas
#+END_SRC
Files:
- data/7a/3b9c4e-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d/w2.pdf

Attach =w2.pdf= and =notes.txt= to =Taxes=:
#+BEGIN_SRC org
,* Taxes                                                         :work:ATTACH:
,** Form 1040
,** Receipts
,* Trips
,** Packing list
,* Ideas
#+END_SRC
Files:
- data/7a/3b9c4e-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d/notes.txt
- data/7a/3b9c4e-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d/w2.pdf

Attach =ticket.pdf= to =Trips=:
#+BEGIN_SRC org
,* Taxes :work:
,** Form 1040
,** Receipts
,* Trips                                                              :ATTACH:
,** Packing list
,* Ideas
#+END_SRC
Files:
- files/trips/ticket.pdf

Attach =my report (final).pdf= and =a?b.txt= to =Taxes=:
#+BEGIN_SRC org
,* Taxes                                                         :work:ATTACH:
,** Form 1040
,** Receipts
,* Trips
,** Packing list
,* Ideas
#+END_SRC
Files:
- data/7a/3b9c4e-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d/a?b.txt
- data/7a/3b9c4e-5d6f-4a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d/my report (final).pdf

Attaching a name that is already in the folder stops with "File already exists" and the path, in Emacs and in the app; neither renames or overwrites.

* Deleting
Deleting all the attachments removes the folder and the =ATTACH= tag.

Attach =w2.pdf= and =notes.txt= to =Taxes=, then delete all:
#+BEGIN_SRC org
,* Taxes                                                                :work:
,** Form 1040
,** Receipts
,* Trips
,** Packing list
,* Ideas
#+END_SRC
Files left: (none)

* Where the app differs from Emacs
- *Link text*: attaching can add an =[[attachment:name]]= link to the text (Settings → Attachments); Emacs only adds the tag.
