#+TITLE: Inline LaTeX
#+STARTUP: showall

Math written between LaTeX delimiters is typeset where you read it: in the outline, in Docs and in HTML export. The file's text is not changed, and editing a line shows the source. LaTeX export writes the math as it is, and ODT export turns each fragment into an embedded formula.

* The delimiters
| Write | Kind | Notes |
|---+---+---|
| =\(...\)= | inline | Org's recommended form; no restrictions |
| =\[...\]= | display | may span lines |
| =$$...$$= | display | may span lines |
| =$...$= | inline | one character is enough (=$x$=); no space just inside the dollar signs |
| =\begin{env}...\end{env}= | display | =\begin= starts a line |

* Inline
The quadratic formula is \(x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\).

Euler's identity, $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$, ties five constants together. A sum such as \(\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}\) stays on the line it is written in.

Greek letters and symbols: \(\alpha + \beta \le \gamma\), \(A \cup B \subseteq C\), \(\forall x \in \mathbb{R}\), \(\infty\).

* Money stays money
A dollar sign next to a number is not math: it costs $5 and $10 total. This is Org's own rule: the opening =$= cannot be followed by a space, comma, period or semicolon, and the closing =$= must be followed by a space, the end of the line or punctuation. So =$a$b= and =$x$-y= stay text.

** Several equations lined up
\begin{align}
E &= mc^2 \\
F &= ma \\
a^2 + b^2 &= c^2
\end{align}
The three equations line up at the =&= marks and are numbered (1) to (3), as KaTeX does for =align=. The =\\= here ends an equation; it is not Org's hard line break.

* When a fragment will not render
KaTeX covers a large part of LaTeX, not all of it. A fragment it cannot read shows its source in a dashed box: \(\notacommand{x}\). KaTeX does not treat =%= as a comment.

* Where the app differs from Emacs
Emacs draws fragments as images made by LaTeX when you toggle previews. Here KaTeX draws them as you read, so spacing can differ slightly.
