This file shows the parts of an outline: keywords, priorities, tags, properties, checkboxes and progress cookies. Emacs 29.3 (Org 9.6.15) reads it the same way; nothing here differs.

Try: tap a TODO badge to step through the sequence, tick a checkbox and watch the cookie in its heading change, and open a heading's ⋯ menu to edit its tags and properties.

* Keywords and priorities
** TODO [#A] Pay the electric bill
** NEXT [#B] Call the dentist
** WAITING Parts for the bike
Waiting on the shop; the keyword is in the =#+TODO:= line at the top.
** DONE [#C] Water the plants
** CANCELED Repaint the fence

* Tags
Tags sit at the end of a heading and are inherited by the headings below it.
** Weekend jobs                                                   :home:
*** TODO Mow the lawn
*** TODO Pick up paint                                           :errand:
** TODO Quarterly report                                          :work:

* Properties
** Laptop
:PROPERTIES:
:SERIAL:   X1-2291
:BOUGHT:   2025-03-14
:CUSTOM_ID: laptop
:END:
A heading's properties live in a drawer. =CUSTOM_ID= gives it a stable link target: [[#laptop][this link]] goes back to it.

* Checkboxes and progress cookies [1/3]
- [X] Buy flour
- [ ] Buy eggs
- [-] Buy milk
  - [X] Whole
  - [ ] Skim

** Packing list [33%]
- [X] Passport
- [ ] Charger
- [ ] Tickets

A heading's cookie counts checkboxes in its own list, or TODO children, depending on what it holds; a =:COOKIE_DATA:= property can choose when it holds both.

** Chores [1/2]
*** DONE Dishes
*** TODO Laundry
