#+TITLE: Checkboxes and progress cookies
#+STARTUP: showall

A cookbook for =[/]= and =[%]= cookies. Toggling a checkbox or a TODO state updates the cookie above it; in Emacs, =C-c C-c= on a cookie does it, and =org-update-statistics-cookies= does the whole buffer. The cookies below are what Emacs 29.3 (Org 9.6.15) leaves after updating this file; the app gives the same.

* Packing [2/4]
- [X] passport
- [ ] charger
- [X] tickets
- [ ] umbrella

* Packing, as a percentage [50%]
- [X] passport
- [ ] charger
- [X] tickets
- [ ] umbrella

* Nested lists [1/2]
- [X] food [2/2]
  - [X] bread
  - [X] cheese
- [ ] drink [1/3]
  - [X] water
  - [ ] tea
  - [ ] coffee

* Heading cookies [2/3]
** DONE Book the flight
** DONE Book the hotel
** TODO Book the train

* Cookies with =COOKIE_DATA= [2/3]
:PROPERTIES:
:COOKIE_DATA: recursive
:END:
** DONE Parent step [1/1]
*** DONE Child step
** TODO Second step
