#+TITLE: Org Timer
#+STARTUP: showall

A timer for meetings, talks and practice. Open the command palette and choose:
- *Start timer* (=C-c C-x 0=, =org-timer-start=): a timer that counts up. It shows as =<0:12:03>= in the modeline.
- *Pause or continue timer* (=C-c C-x ,=), *Stop timer* (=C-c C-x _=).
- *Insert timer list item* (=C-c C-x -=, =org-timer-item=): adds =- 0:12:03 :: = to the end of the text of the heading you are on, and the next one goes under it. Write the note after the =::=.
- The *Timer* button of the text editors (=org-timer=) puts the time at the cursor.
- *Set countdown timer* (=C-c C-x ;=, =org-timer-set-timer=): minutes or =h:mm:ss=. On a heading with an =Effort=, it counts down that effort without asking. At zero, you get a reminder (and the sound of =org-clock-sound=).
- *Shift timer stamps in the text* (=org-timer-change-times-in-region=): moves every stamp in the heading's text by an amount such as =-0:08:26=; leave it empty to make the first one 0:00:00.

* Focus block
:PROPERTIES:
:Effort: 0:25
:END:
*Set countdown timer* on this heading counts down 25 minutes, the effort, and says "Focus block: time out" at zero.
